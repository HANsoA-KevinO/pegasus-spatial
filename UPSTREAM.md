The durable Agent Runtime, Workspace CAS/versioning, memory, compaction, team coordination, model registry, and inherited materials-research code originate from Sci-Pegasus. Pegasus Spatial adds the spatial Project Guide and Skill, persistent external spatial jobs, image/object/world tools, asset lineage, provider isolation, and spatial artifact presentation.
